MediDispatch Emergency medical dispatch, Bengaluru

From the first warning sign to the hospital door.

Wearables watch the patient. The moment readings turn dangerous, the nearest right ambulance is sent by road, and the hospital sees the patient coming, vitals and all.

Dispatcher Watches every case and unit in the city and can overrule any decision.
Hospital Sees who is arriving, how sick they are and when, and takes the handover.
Crew Gets the call on the phone: where to go, who the patient is, what they need.
Patient Knows help is coming and when, and keeps their own health record.
